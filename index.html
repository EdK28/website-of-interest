Meet The Maestro

Jensen Huang is the visionary co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA, the man who turned GPUs into the engines of modern gaming, AI, and accelerated computing. Known for his iconic leather jacket and bold leadership, he has steered NVIDIA from a small graphics startup into one of the world's most influential tech companies—powering everything from real-time ray tracing to today’s AI revolution. Under his leadership, NVIDIA has reshaped entire industries, from gaming and film production to data centers and autonomous vehicles. Widely respected for his long-term vision and relentless innovation, Jensen Huang is often credited as one of the key architects of the modern AI era.
